Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Introduction

Allama Muhammad Iqbal was a world-famous poet, philosopher, and politician. He is known as the National Poet of Pakistan. His inspiring poetry in Urdu and Persian focuses on self-belief, wisdom, and unity, encouraging people to achieve greatness.

Biography and Early Life

He was born on November 9, 1877, in Sialkot. He completed his higher education in philosophy and law in Europe. Throughout his life, he used his writings to inspire people to focus on knowledge, self-respect, and moral values.

Famous Books and Works

  • Bang-e-Dra (The Call of the Marching Bell)
  • Bal-e-Jibril (Gabriel's Wing)
  • Zarb-e-Kaleem (The Rod of Moses)
