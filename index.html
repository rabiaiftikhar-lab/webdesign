Umera Ahmed

Introduction

Umera Ahmed is a renowned Pakistani novelist, screenwriter, and literary figure. She is celebrated for her thought-provoking stories that explore themes of faith, morality, personal growth, and contemporary social issues. Her engaging writing style has earned her a massive fanbase among readers of all ages.

Famous Books and Works

  • Peer-e-kamil
  • Aab-e-Hayat
  • Jannat Ke Pattay
  • Amar Bail
